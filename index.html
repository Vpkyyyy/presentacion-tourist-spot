The Municipality

About Presentacion

Presentacion is a coastal municipality in Camarines Sur, tucked into the Bicol Region on Luzon's southeastern arm. Its eighteen barangays run from quiet upland farms down to the shoreline, which is part of why the town holds two very different postcards at once — the rolling green of Mini New Zealand inland, and the open sea at the View Deck.

Long before it carried its current name, the town was known as Parubcan — a name that still lives on today in Barangay Parubcan. Life here still moves at a small-town pace: rice and coconut farms, fishing boats, and a community that's proud to share its corner of Camarines Sur with visitors.

Scenic Highlands

Mini New Zealand

Mini New Zealand is one of Presentacion's most iconic destinations because it feels like a quiet escape into rolling green hills, fresh air, and wide open countryside. The landscape is shaped by gentle slopes, farm fields, and long stretches of grassy terrain that create a peaceful, almost dreamlike view.

  • Perfect for scenic drives, photo stops, and short nature walks.
  • Best enjoyed in the morning or late afternoon when the light is cooler and the hills look richer in color.
  • Great for travelers who want a relaxing atmosphere away from crowded tourist spots.
Coastal Viewpoint

I Love Presentacion View Deck

I Love Presentacion View Deck is the town's welcoming lookout where visitors can pause, breathe, and enjoy a panoramic view of the sea and surrounding countryside. It is especially popular for relaxing, taking memorable photos, and watching the sunset as the sky changes color over the horizon.

  • Offers a beautiful ocean view and a calm place for family gatherings and quiet moments.
  • Ideal for sunset viewing, taking landscape shots, and enjoying a simple coastal breeze.
  • Popular with tourists, friends, and locals looking for a comfortable scenic stop.
